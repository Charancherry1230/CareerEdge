About FresherXEdge

Built on the Philosophy of Excellence

Bridging the critical gap between academic learning and evolving industry expectations, empowering aspiring engineers to build sustainable careers.

Beyond Training. Towards a Promising Career.

FresherXEdge is another flagship initiative of iGenuine Learning, thoughtfully designed to transform engineering graduates into industry-ready professionals through intensive, domain-specific deep skilling and career-enabling opportunities.

What makes FresherXEdge distinctive is its commitment to go beyond conventional certification. The initiative brings together industry-experienced Subject Matter Experts (SMEs), hands-on learning, real-world engineering applications, continuous competency assessments, and structured interview preparation to build the technical confidence employers expect.

Exclusively Designed For

  • Engineering Graduates: Recent graduates from 2026, 2025, and 2024 passing out batches.
  • Early-Career Professionals: With up to 2–3 years of experience looking to shift into high-growth engineering domains.
  • Eligible Branches: Computer Science (CSE), Electronics & Communication (ECE), Electrical & Electronics (EEE), and allied disciplines.

Eligibility
