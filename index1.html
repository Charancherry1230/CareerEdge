About FresherXEdge

Beyond training.
Towards a career.

FresherXEdge is a flagship initiative of iGenuine Learning, thoughtfully designed to transform engineering graduates into industry-ready professionals through intensive, domain-specific deep skilling and career-enabling opportunities.

What makes FresherXEdge distinctive is its commitment to go beyond conventional certification — bringing together industry-experienced SMEs, hands-on learning, real-world engineering applications, continuous assessments, and structured interview preparation.

Exclusively Designed For

  • ✓ Engineering Graduates: Recent graduates from 2026, 2025, and 2024 passing-out batches.
  • ✓ Early-Career Professionals: With up to 2–3 years of experience looking to shift into high-growth engineering domains.
  • ✓ Eligible Branches: CSE, ECE, EEE, and allied engineering disciplines.
