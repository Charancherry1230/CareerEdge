Eligibility Criteria

Who Can Apply

Designed for engineering graduates and early career professionals aiming for top engineering roles.

  1. 01
    Engineering Degree

    Must be an engineering graduate, preferably 2026, 2025 & 2024 batches from CSE, ECE, EEE, and allied disciplines.

  2. 02
    Academic Performance

    Minimum CGPA of 6.0 with no standing arrears at the time of cohort commencement.

  3. 03
    Academic Consistency

    Minimum 60% aggregate in 10th, 12th, and Diploma (if applicable).
